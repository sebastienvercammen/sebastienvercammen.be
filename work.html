Two ways to work with me

For data teams

  • "Something is broken and nobody can figure out why"A pipeline produces wrong results, behaves unpredictably, or fails intermittently and the obvious fixes didn't hold. I dig until the actual root cause is found, and hand you the report and the fix.
  • "We need to understand how this system actually works"Legacy code, an acquired product, a black-box component nobody fully understands anymore. I document what it really does, from the data flows up.
  • "We need custom tooling that requires understanding first"Pipelines and analysis tools that only work if you understand the system deeply before building. I go deep, then build.

For game studios

  • Crashes & launch stabilityStartup crashes, hardware- or driver-specific failures you can't reproduce in-house: diagnosed at the binary level on your shipped build, no source required.
  • Performance & frame-timeCPU/GPU bottlenecks, stutter, worst-case dips, server tick collapse. I find where the time actually goes and prove the recovery with numbers.
  • Black-box investigationElusive bugs in shipped, closed-source titles: root-caused, reproduced, and reported so your team can act. Several fixes credited or shipped by the studios.

Is this a fit?

A good fit when

  • The obvious fixes already failedThe problem is real and reproducible, and the first plausible explanations didn't hold. You want the actual root cause found, not another guess.
  • You want it proven, not assertedA before/after in numbers anyone can check, measured on the real system, matters more to you than a confident opinion.
  • Understanding has to come firstA black box, legacy code, or a closed binary has to be understood before anything gets built on top of it.
  • Defined scope suits youA defined problem and a defined deliverable, handled async and written-first, fits how you want to work.

Probably not a fit when

  • You need ongoing staffingRegular staffing by the man-hour (a pair of hands to work through a backlog) is a different kind of hire. I take on a defined problem and see it through to a proven result.
  • It has to run on meetingsStandups, live availability, managed time; I work async and written-first, which is what the deep focus depends on.
  • The conclusion is already locked inBeing brought in as a reference expert or a second opinion is welcome. What doesn't work is a conclusion fixed on untested assumptions, with no room for an investigation that might disprove it.

How I work: a defined problem, a defined deliverable, a fixed price. The deliverable is a root-cause report, system documentation, custom tooling, or a working fix, and it speaks for itself.

How the work is done

Root cause, then proof

Whether it's a data pipeline producing wrong numbers or a shipped binary stalling under load, the method is the same.

01

Observe the symptom

Reproduce it and measure it on the real system (frame times, tick rates, wrong rows, stalls) not on a guess or a story about what "should" be happening.

02

Find the actual cause

Trace it to the real root (through profiling, reverse engineering, data analysis, and custom tooling) and keep going past the first plausible explanation until the cause is proven.

03

Prove the result

Intervene, then re-measure. The deliverable is a before/after anyone can verify, plus the documentation to act on it, not a promise.
